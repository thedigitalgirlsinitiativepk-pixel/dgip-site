Questions

Before you sign up.

Is DG·IP really free?

Yes. Roadmaps, videos, e-books, live classes and project reviews are free. Only the optional physical certificate costs Rs. 300, to cover printing and delivery. EasyPaisa payment details on the Get Certified page are coming soon.

Do I need experience?

No. HTML & CSS and Python start at beginner level, and each roadmap guides you phase by phase.

How do I get a certificate?

Build the capstone for your track, email it to us, and once it is approved we print and deliver your certificate. See the Get Certified page for the checklists.

Can someone verify my certificate?

Yes. Every certificate has its own Verification ID, and anyone can email us to confirm it was issued.

Who can join?

DG·IP is built for girls and young women in Pakistan who want to learn tech skills.
