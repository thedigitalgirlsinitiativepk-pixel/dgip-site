Module 05 · Security

Cybersecurity Awareness

Protect yourself, your accounts, and your data — and understand the basics of the field.

5–7 weeks · 3–5 hrs/week No experience needed 4 Learning Phases
Play learning game → All games

On This Page

Cybersecurity affects everyone, not just IT professionals — every phishing email, weak password, and oversharing habit is a real risk. This track starts with protecting yourself online, then opens the door to security as a career path, with practical, hands-on practice throughout.

Practical & Personal Gamified Practice Career Path Overview 4 Phases
Mentor TipEnable two-factor authentication on your email first — it's the account that can reset every other account.

The Roadmap

01

Security FoundationsThe concepts behind every headline

Before diving into tools, understand the small set of core ideas that every security topic builds on.

01
The CIA triadConfidentiality, Integrity, and Availability — the three goals of all security work.
02
Common threat typesMalware, phishing, ransomware, and social engineering, explained simply.
03
How breaches actually happenMost attacks exploit people, not just code — understanding why matters.
04
Key terminologyVocabulary you'll need to follow any security course or article.
Practice ProjectWrite a one-page 'threat glossary' in your own words for five common attack types.
02

Personal Digital HygieneProtecting your own accounts first

The highest-impact security skills are the ones you use on your own devices, every day.

01
Strong passwords & password managersWhy 'P@ssw0rd123' is not strong, and how a password manager fixes this for good.
02
Two-factor authenticationAdding a second lock to every important account.
03
Spotting phishingThe tell-tale signs of a fake email or message trying to steal your credentials.
04
Safe browsing habitsHTTPS, suspicious links, and public Wi-Fi risks.
Practice ProjectAudit your own top 10 accounts: turn on 2FA everywhere possible and replace any reused passwords.
03

Practical SkillsGoing beyond the basics

This phase introduces slightly more technical concepts that show up constantly in security work.

01
Networking basicsIP addresses, routers, and how data actually travels online.
02
VPNs explainedWhat a VPN does and doesn't protect against.
03
Recognizing social engineeringScams that manipulate people rather than exploiting code.
04
Securing a deviceUpdates, device encryption, and app permissions.
Practice ProjectDo a full privacy and security check-up on your own phone: permissions, updates, and backups.
04

Going DeeperA first look at security as a field

For anyone curious about security as a career, this phase is a gentle, guided first step into practical, hands-on practice.

01
Ethical hacking, definedWhat 'white hat' hacking actually means, and how it differs from crime.
02
Capture-the-flag practiceBeginner-friendly, gamified challenges to practice real skills safely and legally.
03
Security career pathsAnalyst, penetration tester, and GRC — an overview of where this can lead.
04
Building a learning habitFollowing security news to keep your knowledge current after this course.
Practice ProjectComplete TryHackMe's beginner-friendly 'Pre Security' path and log what you learned.
